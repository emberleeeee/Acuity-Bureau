About

The gap between
buying AI tools and
using them well.

Acuity Bureau was built for the space between "we bought Figma AI" and "our team actually designs better because of it."

Most design teams are not short on AI tools. They're short on the strategic clarity to use them in a way that's sustainable, governed, and genuinely additive to the work rather than just to the invoice.

Acuity Bureau exists to close that gap. We work as an embedded Fractional AI Design Leader — not a vendor, not a trainer, not an agency. A strategic partner who sits inside your team, understands your context, and helps you build AI design capability that compounds over time.

Our approach is deliberately vendor-neutral. We don't take commissions, we have no preferred partners, and we don't recommend tools because they're popular. We recommend them because they're right for your team, your workflow, and your goals — and we'll tell you honestly when the answer is to use less AI, not more.

The name Acuity Bureau reflects what we believe makes great design leadership: acuity — sharpness of perception, refined judgment, the ability to see clearly what others miss — delivered with the structured, methodical discipline of a bureau. Not a studio. Not an agency. A practice.

How we think
01

Tools don't think. You do.

AI design tools are extraordinary at generating output. They're poor at exercising judgment. Our job is to make sure judgment is never outsourced.

02

Adoption is a behaviour problem.

Most AI tool rollouts fail not because the tools are bad, but because teams weren't given the context, workflow, or permission to use them properly.

03

Speed is a byproduct, not a goal.

The right AI workflow makes your team faster. But we never optimise for speed at the expense of the quality of thought that goes into the work.

04

Governance is a feature.

IP policy, ethical guidelines, and AI use frameworks aren't bureaucracy — they're what allow your team to move fast with confidence and legal clarity.
